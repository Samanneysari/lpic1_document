## Completion rule

Do not mark an objective complete after reading only. Mark it complete when you can:

1. Explain the concept without copying the guide.
2. Recognize the relevant files and commands.
3. Perform its lab safely.
4. Diagnose at least one deliberate failure.
5. Answer the related questions without opening the answer key.
